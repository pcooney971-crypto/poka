Questions

How much notice do you need?

24 hours for up to 20 sandwiches, which is one or two platters. 48 hours for three platters or more. If you need it sooner, email us and we'll see what we can do.

How many platters should I order?

Count on one sandwich per person for lunch. Every sandwich is cut in half, so one platter stretches to 15 or 20 people when there's other food on the table.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes, up to 24 hours before your pickup or delivery time, for a full refund.

Is there anything vegetarian?

The Sicilian Stack, with panko eggplant and fresh mozzarella, and The Amalfi, with lemon ricotta and pear. Both are marked on the list.

What about allergies and gluten?

Some sandwiches have walnuts, dairy, fish or pork, and all are on wheat bread. We don't offer gluten-free bread yet because we can't keep it apart from everything else in our kitchen. Tell us about allergies in your order and we'll take care with it.

Where do you deliver?

About 3 miles from the shop: the South End, Back Bay, Downtown, the Financial District, the Seaport, Fenway and nearby. Further than that, ask us.
