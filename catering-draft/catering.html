Good to Know

How much notice do you need?

24 hours for orders of up to 20 sandwiches (1–2 platters). 48 hours for 30 or more. Need it sooner? Email us and we'll do our best.

How many platters should I order?

Plan on one sandwich per person for lunch. Each sandwich is cut in half, so a platter of 10 also works for about 15–20 people as part of a larger spread.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes. Changes and full-refund cancellations are accepted up to 24 hours before your pickup or delivery time.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. The Sicilian Stack (eggplant caprese) and The Amalfi (lemon ricotta and pear) are both vegetarian. Look for the Veg tag.

Allergies and gluten-free?

Some sandwiches contain walnuts, dairy, fish and gluten. Tell us about allergies in the order notes. We don't offer gluten-free bread right now because we can't guarantee no cross-contact in our kitchen.

Where do you deliver?

Within about 3 miles of the shop: South End, Back Bay, Downtown, the Financial District, Seaport, Fenway and more. Further out? Ask us and we'll see what we can do.
